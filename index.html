01

Sensor testing · Master’s thesis & working student

MEMS inertial-sensor characterisation under controlled ultrasonic excitation

Developed an experimental setup to generate repeatable low-ultrasonic vibration, transfer it to inertial sensors and evaluate frequency-dependent sensitivity.

My contribution

FPGA/DDS excitation, setup integration, automated acquisition, FFT-based evaluation and independent reference measurement.

Validation approach

Laser-vibrometer normalisation, calibrated amplitude comparison and repeatability checks across excitation methods.

Engineering outcome

A reproducible workflow for comparing sensor response across controlled excitation methods and operating conditions.

  • FPGA

Presented as a high-level, NDA-conscious case study; proprietary parameters and data are excluded.
